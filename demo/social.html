How PathCheck checks itself

Five roles that review each other's work. Rules decide who can take a class; NVIDIA Nemotron explains it and reads the policy pages; a checker removes anything it can't prove.

NVIDIA Nemotron, through Meterless Nightshift

Plannernemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b
Turns the question into a plan: target classes, completed courses, term, policy topics. Fast lane.
Analystnemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b
Writes the answer in plain words and cites a record for every claim. Deep lane; Nightshift can route it to nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b.
Checkernemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b
Judges whether each cited policy page really says what the claim says. Judge lane.
Prereq engineno model
Applies the catalog's prerequisite rules exactly, so a model can never overrule them.
